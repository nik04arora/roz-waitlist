About Roz

Investing should not feel like a solo job.

Roz is being built for people who want to build wealth without researching hundreds of funds or making every decision alone.

What we do

You choose your goals and how much you want to invest each month. Roz experts build a mutual fund portfolio around those goals, automate monthly investing, explain what you own, and provide human support when markets move.

How the business works

Roz is powered by Abacus Financial Services Private Limited, an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN holder). Investments distributed through the platform use regular mutual fund plans. Abacus may receive commission from mutual fund houses through the funds’ expense ratios; Roz does not charge the separate advisory or subscription fee described on the current landing page.

Our approach

We believe the useful part of investing technology is not more noise. It is a clear plan, transparent costs, consistent execution, understandable explanations, and access to people when a market decline makes staying invested difficult.
