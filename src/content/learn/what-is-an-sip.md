## SIP meaning in simple terms

A systematic investment plan, usually called an SIP, is a method of investing a chosen amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals. Many investors use a monthly schedule, although the available frequency depends on the fund and platform.

An SIP is not a separate investment product. Your money is invested in the mutual fund scheme you selected, and the value of that investment will rise or fall with the value of the scheme's underlying holdings.

## How an SIP works

On each scheduled date, your instalment is used to purchase units of the mutual fund at that day's applicable net asset value, or NAV. When the NAV is lower, the same instalment generally buys more units. When it is higher, it buys fewer.

This repeated purchasing is commonly called rupee-cost averaging. It can reduce the temptation to time every market move, but it cannot prevent losses or guarantee a profit.

## What an SIP can help with

- Building a consistent investing habit
- Matching investing to a monthly income cycle
- Spreading purchases across different market levels
- Reducing the number of manual decisions you need to make

## What an SIP does not do

An SIP does not make a risky fund safe. Your outcome still depends on the scheme, its costs, the time you remain invested, and market performance. You should choose a fund and contribution amount that fit your goal, time horizon, liquidity needs, and ability to tolerate losses.

## A simple example

Suppose you invest ₹5,000 each month. The number of units purchased will vary as the fund's NAV changes. Over time, your total value will reflect all the units accumulated and the latest NAV—not a fixed or promised rate of return.

## Before starting an SIP

Check the scheme's investment objective, risk level, costs, exit load, and tax treatment. Read the scheme information document and make sure you can keep enough cash available for near-term expenses.

If you are unsure which funds fit your circumstances, consider speaking with an appropriately qualified professional before investing.
