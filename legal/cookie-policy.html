Cookie Policy

This policy explains how rozinvest.com uses cookies and similar browser storage.

Effective and last updated: 19 August 2026

What these technologies are

Cookies are small text files saved by a website in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can remember settings or support website functions.

How this site uses them

The current Roz landing page does not use non-essential advertising cookies. Essential cookies or storage may be used by our hosting and security providers to deliver the site, balance traffic, prevent abuse, and protect forms. If we add optional analytics or advertising technologies later, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent before activating them.

Your controls

You can inspect, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect site availability, security, or form behaviour. Because the current landing page does not activate optional advertising cookies, it does not presently display a cookie-choice banner.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or our service providers change. Questions can be sent to hello@rozinvest.com.
