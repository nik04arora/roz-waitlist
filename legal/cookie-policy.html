Cookie Policy

This policy explains how rozinvest.com uses cookies and similar browser storage.

Effective and last updated: 3 September 2026

What these technologies are

Cookies are small text files saved by a website in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can remember settings or support website functions.

How this site uses them

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and may process information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, referring pages, and visit times. We use this information to measure aggregate website activity and improve the site. We do not use Google Analytics to serve advertising on this website.

Essential cookies or storage may also be used by our hosting and security providers to deliver the site, balance traffic, prevent abuse, and protect forms.

Your controls

You can inspect, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may limit analytics collection, while blocking essential storage may affect site availability, security, or form behaviour.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or our service providers change. Questions can be sent to hello@rozinvest.com.
