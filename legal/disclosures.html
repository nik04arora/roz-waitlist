Important information about Roz’s mutual fund distribution model, compensation, illustrations, and investment risk.

Effective and last updated: 19 August 2026

Business and role

Roz is a digital mutual fund platform powered by Abacus Financial Services Private Limited, an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN holder). Roz is not presenting the general information on this website as personalised investment advice.

Plan type and compensation

Mutual fund transactions distributed through Roz use regular plans. Regular-plan expense ratios include distributor commission. Abacus may receive commission from Asset Management Companies as part of the scheme’s expense structure. You do not pay Roz a separate subscription or advisory fee for the portfolio support described on this landing page.

Risk and returns

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme-related documents carefully.
  • The value of an investment can rise or fall, and loss of principal is possible.
  • Past performance does not guarantee future performance.
  • No website illustration, projected amount, target, or example is a guaranteed return.
  • Investors should evaluate scheme risk, objectives, expenses, liquidity, and suitability for their own circumstances before investing.

Website calculator and educational content

The calculator is an educational illustration based on the values entered and a fixed assumed annual return. It does not represent any specific mutual fund scheme. References to goals, portfolios, or market support are general product descriptions and do not replace scheme documents or individual professional advice.

For commission information, website questions, or grievance support, email hello@rozinvest.com. Additional product-specific disclosures and grievance channels will be presented during onboarding and before any investment transaction.
