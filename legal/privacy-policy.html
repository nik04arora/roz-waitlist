Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Roz Invest and Abacus Financial Services Private Limited (“Roz”, “we”, “us”) handle personal information when you use rozinvest.com or join the Roz waitlist.

Effective and last updated: 19 August 2026

Information we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect the mobile number you provide. We also record the country, the page or form that generated the submission, and basic browser information such as the user-agent string. Our hosting and security providers may process technical logs, including IP address, device information, request time, and requested URL.

How we use information

  • To manage the waitlist and contact you about Roz access or product updates.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • To prevent fraud or abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us host the website, store waitlist records, secure our systems, or communicate with you. These providers may use the information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a corporate reorganisation. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including legal, security, and record-keeping needs. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with your waitlist submission, or ask us to stop contacting you. Some requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal exceptions.

Children

The waitlist is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product and legal requirements evolve. Material changes will be reflected by the date above. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@rozinvest.com.
